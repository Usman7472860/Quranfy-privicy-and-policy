Privacy Policy & Data Security Disclosure

1. Introduction

Welcome to Quranfy By Usman ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy while providing you with an enriching Islamic learning experience, including Holy Quran recitation, Noorani Qaida, Prayer Times, Qibla compass, and daily Islamic content.

This Privacy Policy explains how our Android application (Package Name: com.quranfybyusman) collects, uses, and safeguards your information in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies.

Effective Date: March 2025
Developer Name: Usman
Application Name: Quranfy By Usman

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to deliver and improve our application features:

A. Location Data

If permitted by you, our app accesses your device's approximate or precise location exclusively to calculate accurate local Prayer Times (Namaz timings) and determine the exact Qibla direction towards the Holy Kaaba. Location data is processed locally on your device or sent securely to calculate prayer schedules and is never sold or shared with third parties.

B. Account & Authentication Data

When you register or sign in (for admin, student, or Qari access), we collect your Email Address and Display Name via secure Firebase Authentication. This allows us to maintain your account, sync bookmarks, and connect students with Qari live sessions.

C. Device & Usage Preferences

We store your local preferences (such as selected translation language, font size, calculation methods, and reading bookmarks) locally on your device so that you can enjoy an offline-first experience.

D. Notifications & Cloud Messaging

We use Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to send daily verse alerts, prayer reminders, and live class notifications. You can enable or disable notifications at any time in the app settings.

3. Third-Party Services & SDKs

Our application integrates trusted third-party services that adhere to strict data privacy and encryption standards:

  • Firebase Authentication & Realtime Database: Secure user authentication and live session coordination.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Push notifications for daily Islamic reminders.
  • Google Play Services: Core Android system capabilities and location provider.

4. Data Storage, Security & Offline Mode

We prioritize offline functionality. Quran text, Noorani Qaida lessons, Hadith collections, and Duas are stored locally on your phone. All data transmitted between your device and our backend servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS encryption.

5. Account Deletion & Data Removal Request

In accordance with Google Play's User Data policy, users have the right to request the deletion of their account and all associated personal data.

How to Delete Your Account:
1. You can request account deletion directly within the app under Settings > Legal & Privacy > Request Account Deletion.
2. Alternatively, you can email us at quranfybyusman@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request". Upon verification, your account, live session records, and profile data will be permanently deleted from our servers within 7 business days.

6. Children's Privacy

Our application is suitable for families and children learning the Holy Quran and Noorani Qaida. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately so we can remove it.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
